# Unknotted-to-Knotted Investigation

## Paper claim: 31% success rate

## Our results
- v1 (20% mask, aggressive steps): 3/22 = 14%
- v2 (5% mask, original notebook params): 4/50 = 8%
- Combined unique successes across runs: ~7 proteins total

## Conclusion
The 31% was likely from a small/lucky subset. Our rate across 70+ attempts
is consistently 8-14%. Parameters don't seem to matter much -- the effect
is weak regardless.

For the paper: report honestly, possibly ~10% with confidence interval.
This is still a meaningful result (converting ANY unknotted protein to knotted
is non-trivial) -- just not as high as originally claimed.
